NovaMart Sales & Profitability Dashboard
📊 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed for NovaMart to analyze sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional performance, discounts, and returns.

The project uses a star schema data model with multiple fact and dimension tables to provide interactive business analysis.

🎯 Business Objectives
Analyze overall sales and profitability
Identify high- and low-performing products and categories
Compare sales and profit across regions
Understand the relationship between discounts and profitability
Analyze customer returns and return reasons
Identify actionable business insights

🧩 Data Model

The project follows a star schema consisting of fact and dimension tables, including:

FactSales
FactReturns
FactTargets
DimCustomer
DimProduct
DimDate

Fact tables are connected through shared dimension tables rather than directly to one another.

💡 Key Insights
NovaMart generated ₹25.12M in sales and ₹5.04M in profit, resulting in a 20% profit margin.
Electronics generated approximately ₹22M in sales and ₹4M in profit.
84 units were returned, with Electronics accounting for approximately 68% of returns.
Damaged products were the leading return reason.
🚀 Recommendations
Prioritize high-profit products such as Laptop and Smartphone.
Investigate products with unusually high return volumes.
Reduce damage-related returns through improved packaging and handling.
Monitor discounting strategies to protect profitability.
Focus on profitable growth rather than sales volume alone.
